# 09 · Count the shelf

**Who:** everyone. Staff count their own store's shelf. Owner and manager can count any store and see the reports.
**When:** any time. A few items each day keeps the numbers right.

## Count one item
1. Open **Count**. If you work at more than one store, pick **Which store?** first.
2. Type part of the name, the size or the code in the box (or scan the barcode). Tap the item.
3. Type how many you see on the shelf (kilos for loose rice). Save.
4. The stock changes at once. If it differs from the system, you are asked **Why?** You can **Skip**.

With nothing typed, the box lists **Not counted for the longest time**.

## Count a list
1. **Count** → **Count a list** → **What do you want to count here?** Choose one:
   **Everything here** · **One kind of rice** · **Items not counted lately** · **Pick the items**.
   Then tap **Start counting**.
2. Count each item and type the number. You do not see the system's number while counting. That is on purpose.
3. **Only not counted yet** hides what you already did. **Save for now** keeps your numbers. You can finish later, even on another phone.
4. When you are done, tap **Check the count**.

## Check the count
- Only the items that differ are listed, biggest first. Items that match are just counted.
- **Count again** if the number looks wrong. A big difference is marked **Count it again to be sure**.
- Choose a reason for each difference (for example **Sold but not rung up**, **Spilled**, **Got wet**, **Weevils**, **Sample or given away**, **Scale difference**, **Don't know**).
  The reason is only a label. It does not make a sale or a write-off.
- **A sack may have been opened:** if sacks are down and loose rice is up, tick **Yes, a sack was opened. Record it as a resack.**
- Sign, then tap **Submit the count**.

## After you submit
- The stock changes **at once**. The owner does not have to approve it.
- You see the report: what was counted, what was short or over, and the reason for each.
- **Print count slip** prints it. Items you did not count stay as they are.
- You can have only one open list at a time. **Cancel** a list you started by mistake.

## Also on the Count screen
- **Today's suggested list:** about 10 items to count today, with the reason for each. **Start counting these** starts the list.
- **Quick count: 5 best sellers:** an optional quick count of the 5 items that sold most in the last day (the biggest stock if nothing was sold). Count all five. It posts when you submit. You do not have to do it to close the day.

## Other tabs
- **Report:** past counts for the dates you choose.
- **Write-off:** rice that was spilled, wet, eaten by weevils, a sample or given away. Pick the item, how much, the reason, sign. Over 1% of the item waits for the owner.

## Owner or manager
- **All stores:** every item against every store, with when it was last counted. Red = never counted there; amber = more than 30 days.
- **Needs a look:** big differences, over counts, an item short 3 times in 30 days, many "Don't know". Tap **Open the count**, then **OK, looked at it**. At the end of this tab: the two peso limits and **Count accuracy**.
- At the consignment store (Store 2) a shortage is charged at Store 1's cost at once. The owner can undo it in **Store payable**.
