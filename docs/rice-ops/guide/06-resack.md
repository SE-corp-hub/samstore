# 06 · Resack (repack rice)

**Who:** Owner, manager and staff. Only the porter (kargador) signs. You do not.
**When:** you open a sack into smaller bags, change one kind of rice into another, or open a sack for loose kilos.

## Do a resack
1. Tap **Resack** (bottom bar on a phone). Check the store at the top. Staff tied to a store cannot change it.
2. Tap **Resack now**.
3. Under **Going in**, pick the rice. Each shows what is left, like "— 12 sacks left". Type how many sacks.
4. Under **Coming out**, pick the rice and type how many (**sacks**, or **kg** for loose rice). **+ Another output** adds a kind.
5. Check the box: **In** and **Out** in kilos.
6. Pick the porter under "Porter (kargador) who did it — he signs".
7. Tap **Next**, then **Yes, sign**. The porter signs with a finger on your phone. Tap **Done**.

Splitting a sack: 1 sack of 25 kg in, 5 bags of 5 kg out. Another kind of rice is allowed (a **Variety change** tag shows). Loose kilos: pick the loose rice under **Coming out**.

## The weight check
- Out heavier than in: red, "More came out than went in (over) — weigh again." **Next** stays grey.
- Out lighter by 1% or less: green, "OK, within 1%".
- Out lighter by more than 1%: yellow, "over 1%: the owner will be told". It still goes through (tag **To the owner**).
- Same weight: "Correct — nothing short".

## After Done
- Stock changes **at once**. Nobody waits.
- **Resack saved — RSK-000123**. Tap **Print receipt**. Later use **Receipt** in **Recent resacks**.
- No signal: saved on the phone, done when it is sent.
- Also refused: the same rice in and out, no porter picked, more sacks than the shelf has (a Buffer store may cover that; the owner sees it).
- "No rice with a weight set": tell the owner.

## Made a mistake?
Before **Done**, tap **Back**. A finished resack has no Undo: tell the owner or manager. An older unfinished one under **In resack** can be finished (**Finish**) or **Cancel**led with a reason. The rice goes back to the shelf.
