# 05 · Customer returns

**Who:** owner, manager and counter staff.
**When:** a customer brings rice back. Open **Customer returns** (**More** on the phone, or the menu).

## Record a return
1. Check **Returned to** shows the store that got the rice back.
2. Choose the **Product**.
3. **Which sale was it? (if you know):** the list shows recent sales at this store that had this item, with the number, the date, the customer and how many were bought. Pick the sale. Do not know, or an old sale? Leave **I do not know / not a recent sale**.
4. **How many came back:** tap **+** or **−**, or type. Whole numbers only. With a sale picked you cannot return more than it still has left (the list shows what is already back).
5. Tap **Good — back on the shelf** if you can sell it again. Tap **Damaged** if you cannot.
6. Type the **Reason (why was it returned?)**, three letters or more.
7. Tap **Save return**. It stays grey until all of this is filled in.

## What happens
- **Good:** the shelf count goes up **at once**.
- **Damaged:** the shelf count does **not** go up. It is only written down.
- Either way the owner is told. The note waits in **For your approval** (guide 13). Do not give money back yourself. The owner or manager answers first.
- **Sale picked:** when the owner or manager approves, a credit sale lowers the customer's credit, and a cash or GCash sale makes a note to give the money back. Anything above what the customer still owed is a note to hand back in cash.
- **No sale picked:** approving changes no money. The owner settles it with the customer.
- At a **consignment store** (Store 2), good rice with its sale picked takes that rice's charge off **at once** (guide 11).
- No signature or PIN is needed. No signal: the return saves on the phone and sends later.

## Made a mistake?
This screen has no Undo. If you chose **Good** for wet or broken rice, or typed the wrong product or number, the shelf count is wrong now. Tell the owner or manager at once. Do not record it again.

**Recent returns** (bottom of the screen) lists the last ones.
