# 02 · Sell at the POS

**Who:** owner, manager and counter staff (not sales agents).
**When:** every counter sale, and pre-orders. Open **POS** (bottom bar or menu).

## Make a sale
1. Check **Selling at** shows your store.
2. Customer with special prices? Pick them first in the top box. It locks once rice is added.
3. Tap the rice. Each tile shows the price and the stock in every store (red = 0).
4. Sacks or pieces: type how many. Loose rice: tap **Kilos** or **Pesos**, then type.
5. Tap **Add**. **Edit** or **×** fixes a line.
6. Tap **Pay**, choose how they pay, tap **Done**, then **New sale**.

## Ways to pay
- **Cash:** type **Cash received**. The change shows. If the drawer is closed, tap **Open the cash drawer** first.
- **GCash**, **Maya**, **Bank transfer:** type the reference number.
- **Split:** 2 to 4 parts that add up to the total. No credit in a split.
- **Credit:** choose the customer, or **+ New customer** (name and mobile). The customer signs. A new customer has no limit until the owner sets one. **Over the credit limit** means the sale is refused. The customer pays first on the **Credit** screen.

## Special cases
- **Rice from another store:** under **Taken from**, pick that store. Its count drops at once. **Short** means it has too little.
- **Change the price:** type it under **Price each**, then pick **Discount**, **Unit price** or **Special price (regular customer)**. **Add** stays grey until you do. The owner sees the reason.
- **Hold:** **Hold** parks the sale on this phone only. The rice is not set aside. Later: **Held** → **Resume**.
- **Count is zero or less:** you can still sell. The count goes below zero until the next count. If the owner has a Buffer store, it covers the rice. Its minus number is the owner's list to fix.
- **Is there any left?** shows after a sale empties an item. **Count** = count the shelf and sign. The shelf changes at once. **None left** only closes the box.
- **Paper slip:** turn on **From a paper slip (type in the slip)** on the pay screen.

## After the sale
- **Print receipt.**
- Credit sale: **Text customer** opens your phone's text app with the message ready. Nothing goes out until you press Send there. If the green box says **Sending a text to the customer.**, that is the app's own text. It is test only until the store has a text account.

## Made a mistake?
Before **Done**, use **Back**, **Edit** or **×**. After **Done**, staff cannot undo a sale. Tell the owner or manager. They can void or refund it in **Orders**. Rice brought back: guide 05. No signal: the sale saves on the phone and sends later. Do not enter it twice.

## Pre-orders (**Pre-order**)
1. **New pre-order**. Tap each rice, then **Review order**.
2. Pick the customer (needed). Tap **Save as pre-order**. The rice leaves the shelf count at once.
3. Open ones are in **Pre-Orders** (today only; change **From** and **To**). **Edit**, or **Delete** (the rice goes back).
4. Customer collects: **Finalize** → **Cash** or **Credit (utang)** (customer signs) → **Complete**.
5. **Finalize** tab has **Receipt**. After credit, **Text the customer this transaction** opens your text app.
