# 14 · Weak or no signal

**Who:** everyone who uses the app on a phone.
**When:** the signal is weak or gone while you work.

## What waits on the phone
A sale in **POS**, **Move stock**, **Stock in**, **Customer return**, **Resack**, a payment in **Credit**, a **Write-off**, and counting one item in **Count** are saved on the phone and sent when the signal is back.

1. Do the step as usual. You see "No signal — saved on this phone, it will be sent when the signal returns".
2. A yellow **Waiting to send (1)** button shows in the top bar. Tap it to see the list.
3. Tap **Send now** to try at once. The phone also tries every 20 seconds.
4. Do not enter the same step again.

Until it is sent, shelf counts and money have **not** changed. Let it send before you give the phone to someone else: it goes out under whoever is signed in.

## When the answer is "no"
The button turns red: **Not sent (1)**. Tap it. Each ✕ line says why, for example not enough rice on the shelf, the cash drawer is closed, or "Paper slip #… is already entered".
1. Fix the cause. Do the step again if it is still needed.
2. Tap **Clear the list**.

An expired sign-in or a busy server is not a "no". The step stays in **Waiting to send** and goes after you sign in again.

## What needs a signal
Everything else: **Count a list**, the **Quick count: 5 best sellers**, **Close the day**, **For your approval**, reports, signing in.
- The app opens with no signal, but a screen you open fresh may stay empty. Open what you need while you still have signal.
- "No signal and the phone is full — try again later": nothing was saved or sent. Do it again when you have signal.

## Sales written on paper
Later, in **POS**, switch on **From a paper slip (type in the slip)**. Type the **Slip number** and **Date and time on the slip**, take a **Photo of the slip**, and sign. A slip number goes in once only. A slip over 31 days old is refused: ask the owner.
