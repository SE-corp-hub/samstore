# 08 · Supplier orders and what you owe suppliers

**Who:** Owner and manager. Staff only receive the delivery on **Stock in** (guide 07).
**When:** you order from a supplier, a delivery comes, or you pay a supplier.

On a phone: **More**, then **Supplier orders**. Three tabs: **Orders**, **To reorder** and **Suppliers owe**.

## Make an order
1. On **Orders** tap **+ New order**.
2. Pick the **Supplier**, or **New supplier…** and type the name.
3. Pick **Deliver to** and **Expected**.
4. For each item: choose it, type **How many** (**Kilos** for loose rice) and the **Agreed cost**. **+ One more item** adds a row.
5. Tap **Save order**.

## Price watch
- Once a supplier is picked, each item shows **Last time ₱…** (what that supplier charged on the last order, and the date). The cost box fills with that cost.
- As you type a new cost the line changes to **Up ₱… (…%) from ₱…**, **Down …** or **Same as last time**. Red and bold means up by 5% or more. Ask the supplier why before you save.
- On a saved order the **Cost** column shows **Up …%** or **Down …%** against the same supplier's order before it. Cancelled orders do not count. The first order for a supplier and item shows nothing.

## When the rice comes
- Staff pick the order on **Stock in**, change each number to what really came, and sign.
- Some came: **Partly arrived**. **Left** shows what is still due. All came: **Complete**, by itself.
- More than the order has left: refused. The extra goes in a separate Stock in.
- **Late** (red) shows from the day after **Expected**, if the order is not complete.
- The red button says **Close order** if some rice came (what arrived stays, the rest is no longer expected) or **Cancel order** if none came. You confirm first.
- **Waiting** and **All** choose which orders show.

## To reorder
The **To reorder** tab (the number on the tab is how many items) lists what would run out within 7 days at the pace of the last 2 weeks. All stores and the warehouse are added together. Buffer stores are left out.
1. Read each line: **Left**, **Sells a day**, **Days left**, **On order** and **Order**. **Out** (red) means none left, **Order now** (yellow) means under 3 days, **Soon** means under 7. **Order** is enough for 14 days of selling, with what is already on order taken off. Loose rice is in whole kilos.
2. **Last supplier** shows who you ordered the item from last time, and at what cost.
3. Untick any item you do not want now.
4. Tap **Start an order with the … ticked items**. The **Orders** tab opens with the form filled in. The supplier is filled only when every item had the same last supplier. Change anything, pick the supplier if it is empty, then **Save order**.
An item with no sales in the last 2 weeks is never listed, because the app cannot tell how fast it sells. It is a suggestion only. Nothing is ordered until you save.

## What you owe
1. Open **Suppliers owe**. **You owe suppliers** is what arrived, at the agreed cost, minus what you paid. A delivery with no order adds nothing. **Still on order** is not owed yet.
2. **Record payment**: type **Amount paid**, tap **Save payment**. Refused if it is more than you owe plus what is still on order.
3. **Statement** lists deliveries and payments with the balance. **Print** prints it.

## Made a mistake?
Wrong payment: in the **Statement** tap **Undo**, then **Undo payment**. A new line takes it off and the old line stays. A payment can be undone once. A delivery cannot be undone.
