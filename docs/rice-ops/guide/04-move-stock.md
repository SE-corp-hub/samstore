# 04 · Move stock between stores

**Who:** Owner, manager and staff. A staff phone tied to one store always moves rice **into** that store.
**When:** rice goes from one store (or the warehouse) to another.

## Move rice
1. Open **Move stock** (phone: **More**, then **Move stock**).
2. Under **Moving to**, tap the store that gets the rice. A phone tied to a store has it chosen already.
3. Under **Taking from**, tap the store the rice leaves.
4. Pick the rice under **Product**.
5. Under **How many**, type the number or tap **+** and **−**. Whole numbers only.
6. Read the line "Move 5 × … Both counts change now." Fix it if it is wrong.
7. Tap **Move now**.

## What happens
- **Both** counts change at once. No signature on the screen. Nobody has to approve.
- You see **Moved — TR-000123**. **Print receipt** has blank lines for **Moved by** and **Received by**.
- **Recent moves** lists past moves. **Receipt** prints one again.
- No signal: **saved on this phone**. The counts change only when it is sent.

## What the app refuses
- The same store twice ("Pick two different places"). A number below 1.
- Rice between two stores that are not yours. Only the owner or manager can do that.
- More than the store has: "Not enough … Count again or lower the number." If your shop has a Buffer store, the move goes through anyway and the missing part comes from the Buffer. The owner sees it on the Store report. So count the shelf first.

## Made a mistake?
There is no Undo. Do a new move the other way with the same number. A phone tied to a store cannot send rice out: ask the owner or manager.
