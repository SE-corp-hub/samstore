## Three rules for everyone

1. **Use your own PIN and your own signature.** Your name is on everything you sign. Never sign for someone else, and never share your PIN.
2. **Never fix a mistake by entering the same thing again.** If something signed was wrong, tell the owner or manager. They undo or void it, with a reason, and the record stays.
3. **Count what you really see.** Type what is on the shelf, even if you think the system is right. Never guess a number to make it match. If you do not know why it is different, choose **Don't know**.

## Who sees what

Staff see their own store and never see cost or profit. The owner and manager see all stores, cost, profit, payments between stores, and the owner-only parts of each guide (marked **Owner or manager**).
