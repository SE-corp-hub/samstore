# 01 · Sign in with your PIN

**Who:** everyone who works in the store.
**When:** at the start of work, when someone else takes the phone, and on a new phone.

## First time: the temporary password
1. Open the app in Chrome.
2. Type your mobile number in **Phone or email** and the temporary password in **Password**. Tap **Sign in**.
3. On **You must change your password**, type a **New password** (at least 8 characters). Type it again in **Retype new password**.
4. Tap **Confirm**. You are in.

## Every day: your PIN
1. Tap **Staff: log in with PIN**.
2. Type your mobile number in **Mobile number**. The phone remembers it.
3. Tap your 4 numbers on the big pad. The 4th number signs you in. There is no OK button.
4. Typed a wrong number? Tap the erase key, or **Clear**.

Your PIN is 4 numbers, made by the owner. It cannot be 1111 or numbers in a row like 1234. To change it, ask the owner.

## If it does not work
- **Wrong number or PIN. 3 tries left.** Check your number and try again.
- 5 wrong tries lock the PIN for 15 minutes. Wait, or ask the owner to unlock it. Your password still works.
- **No internet. Try again.** Find a better signal.
- Back at the sign-in screen by surprise? The owner changed your PIN or removed you, or about 30 days passed. Sign in again.

## Signing out, new phone
- The phone stays signed in until you tap **Log out**. It is in the menu (three lines, top left) and as the small exit button at the top right.
- Handing the phone to the next person: open the menu, tap **Switch user (PIN)**.
- New or lost phone: tell the owner. A new PIN signs out every old phone at once.
- Icon on the Home screen: in Chrome tap the three dots, then **Add to Home screen** (or **Install app**).

## Finding your way
- Bottom bar: **POS**, **Pre-order**, **Resack**, **Credit**, **Orders**, **More**. The top of the screen names where you are.
- Staff: **More** opens **What do you need to do?** with big buttons, the **Stock** screens, and **Other screens** for the rest.
- Owner and manager: **More** opens the full menu.
