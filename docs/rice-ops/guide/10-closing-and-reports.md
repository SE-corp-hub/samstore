# 10 · End of day: closing and reports

**Who:** staff, manager and owner. Staff close the day. The owner and manager parts are at the bottom.
**When:** at closing time, and when you need the numbers of a day, week or month.

## Close the day
1. Open **Reports & closing** (**More**, then **Other screens**). Stay on the **Closing** tab.
2. On your store's card tap the green **Closing** button. It shows only for today, until the day is closed.
3. **1. Count the cash in the drawer.** Type the amount on the pad. The screen says **Correct**, **Short** or **Over**. The change fund is not counted.
4. **2. Count the top 5.** Tap **Count the top 5 now** and count (guide 09). Stopped half way? Tap **Continue the count**. It says **Done** at the end.
5. **3. Still open.** Trips or resacks not finished. **None.** is good. Finish any resack first.
6. **4. Your signature.** Sign with your finger.
7. Tap **Close the day**. It stays grey until the cash is typed and you have signed. You see "The day is closed — sent to the owner".

## What happens
- The day's numbers lock. The owner sees your cash count next to what it should be.
- The top 5 count changes the shelf numbers at once.
- **Close the day** does not check steps 2 and 3. Do them first.

## Made a mistake?
- A closed day cannot be reopened in the app. Tell the owner.
- Sales after closing still go through. They show in a red **After closing** note, not in the closed numbers.
- **Choose a location**: the owner has not set your store. Ask the owner.

## Store report
Open **Store report** and pick your store. Tabs:
- **Stock left**: what is on the shelf, with price and worth. **Out** and **Low** are marked.
- **Sales**: the total; Cash, GCash, Credit; best sellers.
- **All records**: every sale, stock change and money in.
- **End of day**: one day. **Close the day…** goes to the closing above.

Dates: tap **Today**, **This week**, **This month** or **This year**, or fill **From** and **To**. Only **Sales** has all four.
Print: **Print slip**, **Print / PDF (A4)** (then choose **Save as PDF**), or **Print**. See guide 15.

## Owner or manager
Staff never see cost or profit. You also see **Profit**, **Best by profit**, **Worth (cost)**, the **Compare stores** tab, **One store / All stores**, and what a store owes the main store.

**Reports & closing** has more tabs (**Excel** saves a file): **Stock Live** (every store's rice now), **Today** (all stores: sales, profit, expenses, net profit), **This month**, **Short** (count shortages), **Trace** (search a document), **Alerts**, **Stock check**, **Stock book**.
