# 16 · Weekly summary and stock movement (owner or manager)

**Who:** Owner and manager only.
**When:** once a week for the **Week** page. **Movement** when you want to know where the rice went.

Both are tabs in **Reports & closing** (**More**, then **Other screens**). They only read. Nothing here changes stock or money.

## Week
One page for the week. Open **Reports & closing**, then the **Week** tab.

1. **Week ending** starts on today. It shows the 7 days ending on that day, against the 7 days before. Pick another day and tap **Show**.
2. **Sales:** the total, how it compares with last week (**up** or **down** …%, or "no sales last week"), and a line for each store: **This week**, **Last week**, **Change**, **Sales** (how many).
3. **Credit:** given this week, collected this week, what customers owe now and how many customers owe.
4. **Consignment store** (Store 2): what it owes, what it paid this week, the date of its last payment and how many days ago. A red **Payment late** shows if it owes and has not paid for 7 days. **Open Store payable** goes to guide 11.
5. **Suppliers:** what you owe, what is still on order, **late order** count and who is owed most. **Open Supplier orders** goes to guide 08.
6. **Counts this week:** how many counts, how much was short and over in pesos, and **Reason** by reason (guide 09). A big **Don't know** is worth a talk with the staff.
7. **Running out:** items that would run out within a week. They are the same as the **To reorder** tab in guide 08.
8. **Print** prints the page on A4.

Profit is at cost. The numbers come from the same reports as **Store report** and **Store payable**, so they should agree with them.

## Movement
Every item at one store, or all places together, between two dates.

1. Pick **Where**, **From** and **To**, then tap **Show**. It opens on the last 7 days. It allows at most 93 days.
2. Each row is one item: **Start**, then **Bought**, **Moved in**, **Moved out**, **Sold**, **Resack**, **Count fix**, **Returns / cancelled**, **Other**, and **End**. **Start** plus the columns equals **End**. Green is rice in, red is rice out, a dash is nothing.
3. Sacks are in pieces. Loose rice is in kilos (shown as **(kg)**). **Total kilos** adds everything up in kilos.
4. **Print** (A4, sideways) and **Excel** (a file you open in Excel) use the same numbers.

Notes:
- **Moved in** and **Moved out** are moves between stores. With **All places together** they cancel out.
- **Returns / cancelled** is rice that came back from a customer, or from a cancelled sale.
- **Other** is anything that fits none of the columns. A large number there is worth asking about.
- An item that did not move and has nothing on the shelf is left out.
- **Start** and **End** are worked out from the stock now and the moves since. If **Stock check** says an item's shelf and its record disagree, fix that first.
