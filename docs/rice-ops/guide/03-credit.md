# 03 · Credit: customers who owe, and payments

**Who:** owner, manager and counter staff. Limits and special prices: owner or manager only.
**When:** adding a credit customer, taking a payment, texting or printing a balance. Open **Credit**.

## Add a customer
1. Tap **New** next to the search (at the POS: **+ New customer**).
2. Type **Name** and **Mobile number**. Tap **Add**.

A new customer has **no limit** until the owner sets one.

## Find a customer
The chips under the search pick who shows: **Owes**, **Over 30 days** (the overdue days set in Settings), **Near limit** or **All**. Each chip shows how many.
Held sideways, the list stays on the left and the customer you tap opens on the right. Upright, the customer opens full screen; **← Customers** goes back.

## Take a payment
1. Tap the customer, then **Receive payment**.
2. **How much did they pay?** Tap **All**, a quick amount (₱100, ₱200, ₱500, ₱1,000) or type it on the pad. A part payment is fine. More than they owe shows **Too much**.
3. **Paid by:** **Cash** or **GCash** (type the reference number).
4. **Signatures:** staff signs (needed). The customer's signature is optional.
5. The bar at the bottom shows what is paid and what is left, and what is still missing. Tap **Receive ₱…**. The balance goes down at once. Stock does not change.
6. A green box says **Received**. Tap **Print receipt** for the customer's slip or **Text receipt** to open your text app.

## Text or print the balance
- **Text the balance:** your phone's text app opens with the message ready. Press Send there. Nothing goes out until you do. No valid mobile number? The number is missing or wrong.
- **Print statement** (slip) or **Statement (A4)**.
- **History** shows each utang and payment, newest first, with the balance after it. **Texts** under it opens this customer's texts.
- **Texts** tab: **Text opened** means the text app opened. The app cannot tell if you pressed Send. **Failed** has **Send again**. The app does not send texts by itself: the shop uses your phone's own text app, so only **Text the balance** and **Text receipt** really reach people.

## At the limit
The badge says **At limit — pay first**. The POS refuses a credit sale that would go over the limit (**Over the credit limit**). Take a payment first. Then the customer can buy on credit again. Staff cannot raise a limit.

## Made a mistake?
This screen has no Undo for a customer or a payment. Tell the owner or manager. Do not enter it again.

## Owner or manager
- Open the customer and tap **Customer settings** at the bottom.
- **Limit:** type **Limit ₱**, tap **Save limit**. **No limit** removes it. 0 means no credit at all.
- **Special prices:** choose a product, type the price, tap **Save price**. **Remove** deletes it. At the POS, pick the customer first and the prices show on the rice.
- **Settings** tab: switches for the automatic texts and reminders. The shop has no text account, so these are only recorded in the Texts tab, not sent.
