# 11 · Store payable and the consignment store (Store 2)

**Who:** Owner and manager only.
**When:** every week, when a store pays, or when a count finds rice missing.

A consignment store (Store 2) sells Store 1's rice and owes Store 1's cost for each sale. The app adds that charge by itself. A voided sale takes it off again. Good rice returned to Store 2 with its sale picked on **Customer returns** (guide 05) takes that charge off too, at once.

On a phone: **More**, then **Store payable**.

## Each store's card
It shows "**Store 2 owes** ₱…", **Charged** and **Paid**. For a consignment store you may also see red **Weekly payment due** (it owes, and has not paid for 7 days) and yellow **Count the shelf this week** (no count for 7 days).

1. **Record payment**: type **Amount paid**, tap **Save payment**. Not more than it owes.
2. **Print statement**: every charge and payment, with the balance.
3. **Check shelf**: per item **Supplied**, **Sold**, **Returned**, **Moved back**, **Resack from count**, **Count fix**, **On shelf**, **Cost owed**. A second table gives kilos per kind of rice. **Gone by count** is rice a count found missing. A red **Count fix** also means missing.
4. **Weekly sales total** (consignment store only): shows what the rice that left since the last total is worth at the selling price. Type **Collected** and tap **Save**. A negative **Difference** means it collected less than the rice is worth. Ask why.

## A count finds rice missing at Store 2
The count posts at once. Each shortage is charged to Store 2 **automatically**, at Store 1's cost. Wrong? Undo it (below). The shortage goes back to **To charge**.

## Charge by hand
- **To charge — shortages found by counts:** not yet charged to anyone. Pick **Charge to**, then **Charge** or **Charge all**. Leave out a loss that is not a store's fault.
- **Add a charge by hand:** store, item, how many, **Charge**. Refused if the item has no cost.

## Made a mistake?
In **All entries** tap **Undo** on the line and type the reason (it is needed). Nothing is deleted: a new line cancels it. A charge cannot be undone while payments cover it. Undo the payment first.
