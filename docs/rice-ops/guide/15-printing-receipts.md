# 15 · Printing receipts

**Who:** everyone who prints. Staff set up in **Counter & printer**. Owner and manager use the same card at the top of **Settings**.
**When:** the first time on a phone, and when the printer stops answering.

## Set up (once on each phone)
Settings stay on this phone only.
1. Open **Counter & printer** (**More**, then **Other screens**). Find **Printer (this phone)**.
2. **Paper width:** tap **58 mm** (small roll) or **80 mm**.
3. **Print the receipt right after each sale** makes printing automatic. It also prints after Move stock, Resack, Stock in and a count. **Two copies (customer and store)** prints two.
4. Bluetooth printer: turn it on, switch on **Print straight to my Bluetooth printer (no print window)**, tap **Connect printer**, and pick your printer in Chrome's list. You see "Connected: …".
5. Tap **Print a test line**. A short line should print.

No Bluetooth printer? Leave that switch off, tap **Test print**, and pick the printer in the print window (or **Save as PDF**).

## Print a receipt
Tap **Print receipt** right after the job:
- **POS**: on the **Sold** screen. An older sale: **Orders**, the small receipt button, **Print (1 copy)**.
- **Move stock**, **Resack**, **Stock in**: after saving. Later: **Receipt** in **Recent moves**, **Recent resacks**, **Recent stock in**.
- **Count**: **Print count slip** after you submit; **Print count sheet** while counting.

Printing never changes stock or money. Print again as often as you like.

## The printer does not answer
1. Turn it on, keep it close, tap **Connect printer** again.
2. Tap **Print a test line**. "Sent to the printer" is good. "Did not print: …" is not.
3. Tap **Check my printer**, pick the printer, and read the **RESULT** line. Tap **Copy the report** and send it to the owner.
4. Not usable, or the printer never shows in Chrome's list? It is an older kind of Bluetooth that Chrome cannot use. Switch **Print straight to my Bluetooth printer** off and print through the print window. If the phone does not list the printer, install the printer's own print service app and pick it there.
5. "Bluetooth print did not work … using the print window": print from the window that opens.
6. "This browser cannot print straight to Bluetooth. Use Chrome.": open the app in Chrome.

On a Bluetooth printer the peso sign prints as P.
