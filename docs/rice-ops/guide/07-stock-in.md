# 07 · Stock in (rice arrives)

**Who:** Owner, manager and staff. The person who receives the rice signs.
**When:** a delivery arrives at your store.

## Receive a delivery
1. Open **Stock in** (phone: **More**, then **Stock in & review**).
2. Pick the store at the top. A phone tied to a store has it chosen already.
3. Supplier order: pick it under **Is this a delivery for an order?** The supplier and the cost come from the order. You never see the cost. Change each number to what really came. **×** removes an item that did not come.
4. No order: leave **No order — enter it myself**. Type the **Supplier (if any)**. Pick each item and type **how many** (kilos for loose rice). **+ One more** adds a row.
5. Sign in **Signature of the receiver**.
6. Tap **Receive**.

## Item not in the list
1. Tap **+ New item (not in the list)**.
2. Type the **Name**. Tap **Sack**, **Kilo** or **Piece**. For a sack, type the kilos in one sack. Type the **Selling price**. Tap **Add**.
3. It goes straight into Products. The owner does not check it. It has no stock yet: type how many arrived and receive as above.

## What happens
- Stock goes up **at once**. You see **Received — IN-000123**. Tap **Print receipt**. **Recent stock in** has a **Receipt** button to print again.
- No signal: saved on the phone. Stock goes up when it is sent.

## What the screen refuses
- **Receive** stays grey until every row has an item and a number, and you have signed.
- More than the order has left: red, "More than is left on the order". Put the extra in a second Stock in with no order.
- Sacks must be whole numbers.

## Made a mistake?
There is no Undo. Tell the owner or manager. Counting the shelf (guide 09) puts the number right.

## Owner or manager
With no order, each row has a **₱ cost** box, and **New item** has **Unit cost (what you pay)**. Both are optional. A cost you type becomes the item's latest cost.
