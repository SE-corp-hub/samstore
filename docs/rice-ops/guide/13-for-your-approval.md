# 13 · For your approval (owner or manager)

**Who:** owner and manager. The owner decides everything. A manager decides only customer returns. On other lines a manager sees "The owner decides this one."
**When:** something waits for your yes or no. Open **For your approval** in the menu.

## What waits here today
- **Customer return** (grey label **REFUND**): staff saved a return and you answer. Owner or manager.
- **Short by more than 1%**: a resack that came out more than 1% lighter. Owner only.
- **Write-off**: rice written off, more than 1% of that item's stock. Owner only.
- **Credit over the limit**: older held sales only. New credit sales over the limit are refused at the POS now. Owner only.
- Counts do not wait here. They post when staff submit them.

## Decide
1. The list opens on **Waiting**. **All** shows past ones.
2. Read the line. Tap **Approve** or **Decline**.
3. Declining needs a **Reason** (3 letters or more).
4. Type **Your PIN** (your own 4 numbers).
5. Tap **Approve** or **Decline** again. You see "Approved" or "Declined".

No PIN? Tap **No PIN yet, or forgot it? Make / change my PIN**. Type your password (or current PIN) and a **New PIN (4 digits)**, then tap **Save PIN**.

## What happens to the stock
- **Return:** good rice is on the shelf already. The line says "from order …" when staff named the sale.
  - **Approve, sale named:** a credit sale lowers the customer's credit (the customer gets a text). A cash, GCash or other sale makes a refund note: hand the money back yourself. More than the customer still owed is also a note to hand back.
  - **Approve, no sale named:** nothing changes in the money. Settle it with the customer yourself.
  - **Decline:** no money changes. The rice already moved when staff saved it.
- **Resack, write-off:** the stock changed when staff saved it. Your answer is only the record.
- **Credit over the limit:** **Approve** takes the rice off the shelf and adds the credit. **Decline** puts the rice back and cancels the sale.

## Rules
- Each line is decided **once**: "This was already decided".
- A manager cannot approve their own request: "You cannot approve your own request". Ask the owner. The owner can.
- "Set a PIN first before deciding". "Wrong PIN".
- There is no Undo. Check before you tap.
