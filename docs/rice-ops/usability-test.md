# Usability check: one older staff member, their own phone

**Done when:** one older staff member does the five tasks below **without help**, on their own phone.

This check needs a real person, so it cannot be automated. The owner runs it once, in about 25 minutes, in a quiet hour. Run it on the beta site first, then again on the real app before go-live.

## Before you start
1. On the staff member's own Android phone, open the store link in Chrome and sign in with **their own PIN**.
2. Chrome menu ⋮ → **Add to Home screen** (or **Install app**). The **Tindahan** icon appears on the home screen. Open the app from there.
3. Prepare at their store: a few sacks of one kind of rice (for example Sinandomeng 25 kg) and some loose rice on the shelf, a customer on the **Credit** list with a limit, and a second store to move rice to.
4. You only watch. Do **not** point at the screen or explain. Write down every place where they stop for more than about 10 seconds or ask a question.

## The five tasks (read each one aloud, once)
| # | Say this | Done when |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | "Sell 2 kilos of Sinandomeng for cash. The customer gives ₱200." | The sale is completed and the screen shows the correct change. |
| 2 | "Move 2 sacks of Sinandomeng from this store to Store 2." | **Move stock** shows the move under Recent moves and both stores' counts changed. |
| 3 | "Make five 5 kg bags out of one 25 kg sack." | **Resack** finishes with a receipt, and kilos in equal kilos out. |
| 4 | "Juan paid ₱500 on his credit." | **Credit** shows the payment and Juan's balance is ₱500 lower. |
| 5 | "Count the Sinandomeng 25 kg on your shelf. You see 3 sacks." | **Count** saves it and the shelf number is 3. |

## Score
- **Pass:** all five done without help. Small pauses are fine.
- **Needs work:** any task where they asked for help or gave up. Write down the screen and the word or button that confused them and send it to Claude. The wording or the button is changed, then repeat with the same person.

## Also check on that phone
- The text and numbers are easy to read in daylight, with their usual glasses.
- Every button is easy to hit with one thumb.
- Turn on **Airplane mode** and make a sale. The screen says it is saved on the phone, and the top bar shows **Waiting to send (1)**. Turn Airplane mode off. Within about a minute the badge goes away and the sale shows in the reports, once.
- Close Chrome completely and open the app again from the home screen. They are still signed in. They only sign in again after tapping **Sign out**.
