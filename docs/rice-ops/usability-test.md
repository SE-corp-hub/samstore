# M10 usability check — one older staff member, their own phone

**Done when (plan):** one older staff member completes Benta, Ilabas, Resack and Bayad sa utang **without help**, on their own phone.

This check needs a real person — it cannot be automated. The owner runs it once, in about 20 minutes, on a quiet hour.

## Before you start
1. On the staff member's own Android phone, open the store link in Chrome and log in with **their own PIN** (Palit user).
2. Chrome menu ⋮ → **Add to Home screen** (or **Install app**). The green bowl icon "Tindahan" appears on the home screen. Open it from there.
3. Prepare: 1 sack in stock at their store, a customer on the Utang list with a limit, a kargador on the list, and a release (Ilabas) waiting at their store.
4. You watch only. Do **not** point at the screen or explain. Write down every place they stop for more than ~10 seconds or ask a question.

## The four tasks (read each one aloud, once)
| # | Say this | Done when |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | "Magbenta ka ng 2 kilo na Sinandomeng, cash, ₱200 ang ibinayad." | The screen shows **Naibenta na** and the correct sukli. |
| 2 | "May ilalabas para sa Store 1. Ilabas mo, kasama si Mang Boy." | The trip shows **Nailabas na**; both signed. |
| 3 | "Gawin mong limang 5 kg ang isang sakong 25 kg." | **Tapos ang resack**, kilos in = out (or the loss shown). |
| 4 | "Nagbayad si Juan ng ₱500 sa utang niya." | **Natanggap ₱500 — natitira …**; a text is queued. |

## Score
- **Pass:** all four done without help. Small pauses are fine.
- **Needs work:** any task where they asked for help or gave up — note the screen and the word that confused them, and send it to Claude; the wording or the button is changed, then repeat with the same person.

## Also check on that phone
- Text and numbers are easy to read without glasses (or with their usual glasses) in daylight.
- Every button is easy to hit with one thumb.
- Turn on **Airplane mode**, make a sale: it says **Walang signal — naka-save sa phone**; the top bar shows **Naghihintay ipadala (1)**. Turn Airplane mode off: within ~20 seconds the badge disappears and the sale appears in Ulat.
- The **English** button switches the menu and the signing screens to English; tap again for Taglish.
